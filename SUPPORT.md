# Support

For questions and reproducible bugs, open an issue in [veetaw/remote-sync-workspace](https://github.com/veetaw/remote-sync-workspace/issues).

Include your extension and VS Code versions, operating system, protocol, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and actual behavior. Share only redacted logs or diagnostics. Remove hostnames, usernames, paths, credentials, and project content that you do not want to make public.

For uncertain transfers, pause uploads, preserve the local workspace and extension state, inspect the server, and follow the recovery commands in the README. Do not delete metadata to clear a blocked queue.

For suspected vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.
